## Publish Beauty Nails Αργυρούπολη

Before going live, update the site's metadata so Google and social previews show the right info, then publish.

### Steps

1. **Update SEO metadata** in `src/routes/__root.tsx` and/or `src/routes/index.tsx`:
   - Title: "Beauty Nails Αργυρούπολη — Μανικιούρ, Πεντικιούρ & Αισθητική"
   - Meta description: Greek summary mentioning services, location (Λεωφ. Κύπρου 58, Αργυρούπολη), and phone.
   - Open Graph tags (og:title, og:description, og:type)
   - Twitter card tags
   - Favicon set to the Beauty Nails logo

2. **Publish** the project to its Lovable URL. After publishing you can optionally:
   - Rename the Lovable subdomain
   - Connect a custom domain from Project Settings → Domains
